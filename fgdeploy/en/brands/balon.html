About Balon Valve

Founded
1965
Headquarters
Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA
Sector
Industrial ball valves

Established in 1965 in Oklahoma City, Balon Corporation has built its reputation on the design and manufacture of heavy-duty ball valves for demanding service conditions. All Balon valves are manufactured in the USA and meet API 6D (pipeline valves) and API 6A (wellhead and tree equipment) standards, making them a preferred choice for upstream, midstream and downstream Oil & Gas applications.

Balon's product range includes floating ball valves, trunnion-mounted ball valves and through-conduit gate valves in sizes from ½" to 36", covering pressure classes from ANSI 150 to ANSI 2500. Their valves are widely used in high-integrity pipeline isolation, process headers and emergency shutdown applications.

Key strengths

  • USA-manufactured, API 6D and API 6A certified
  • Trunnion and floating ball designs for full pressure range
  • Available with fire-safe and anti-static certification
  • Sizes from ½" to 36", ANSI 150 to ANSI 2500
