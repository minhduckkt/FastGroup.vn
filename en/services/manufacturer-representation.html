Fast Group helps global industrial manufacturers build a credible local presence, generate qualified leads and grow revenue in Vietnam's expanding industrial market.

The opportunity & the barriers

A growing market — and the real barriers standing between you and it

Vietnam is in the middle of a sustained industrial expansion. Oil & gas projects, power plants, petrochemical complexes, EPC mega-projects and manufacturing parks continue to attract investment at scale, driven by both domestic energy demand and the shift of global supply chains into Southeast Asia. For international industrial equipment manufacturers, the addressable market is substantial and growing.

Yet the gap between a strong product and a successful order in Vietnam is real. Foreign manufacturers face a combination of structural barriers that are difficult to bridge from abroad: a technical language gap that causes mis-specification; complex import procedures requiring specialist documentation; a culture of in-person, relationship-driven procurement; an expectation of on-site technical support; and the inherent caution of procurement departments at refineries, power plants and EPC contractors who are accountable for the equipment they specify. An English-language website and a price list sent from overseas is rarely enough.

What Vietnamese industrial customers actually need is a credible local partner: one who understands the product, speaks the technical language, responds to RFQs quickly, manages import and documentation end to end, and is present when questions arise. That is the role Fast Group takes on for the manufacturers it represents.

What Fast Group does for manufacturers

Six pillars of local market representation

We manage the full cycle from market entry to repeat orders — so your team focuses on product development while we develop your Vietnam revenue.

Market entry & local presence

We establish your brand's presence in Vietnam: a localised brand website, correct Vietnamese product naming and positioning, and a local point of contact that buyers, engineers and procurement teams can reach during Vietnam business hours.

Lead generation & customer development

We proactively approach target customers — plant operators, EPC contractors, maintenance teams, procurement managers — introduce your brand and products at the technical level, and build the relationships that convert awareness into purchase orders.

Localised product information & Vietnamese SEO

Product datasheets, application notes and selection guides are translated and adapted for Vietnamese industrial readers. Where applicable, we create dedicated Vietnamese-language web presence to capture search demand and build brand credibility with local decision-makers.

RFQ handling & quotation

Every enquiry — from a project engineer, procurement officer or maintenance team — receives a fast, accurate technical response. We confirm model applicability, verify supply, prepare a formal quotation and follow the opportunity through to order closure.

Import, customs & documentation

We manage international logistics, Vietnamese customs clearance, import taxes and the specialised paperwork industrial projects require: CO/CQ certificates, datasheets, packing lists and any project-specific technical documentation. Delivery to site or contractor warehouse is coordinated as part of the service.

After-sales coordination & market feedback

After delivery, Fast Group remains the local contact for warranty matters, spare-parts requests and post-sale technical questions. We also collect and relay structured market feedback — competitor pricing, application trends, customer requirements — so you can refine your Vietnam strategy with real data.
