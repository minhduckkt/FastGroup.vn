Mô tả gốc của SpotSee (tiếng Anh)

World-Class Monitoring SpotSee is trusted globally for delivering reliable impact monitoring solutions. The SpotBot® GL tracks impacts ranging from 3.5 to 100G, making it versatile for monitoring assets such as medical devices, automotive components, and power generation equipment. Its precise temperature and humidity sensors are ideal for safeguarding sensitive items like biotech, pharmaceutical, and diagnostic products. Long Battery Life & Accurate Location Reporting The SpotBot GL ensures accurate location tracking using three advanced technologies: GPS, WiFi signal proximity, and cellular tower triangulation. It also supports geofencing, alerting users when an asset reaches its destination. Global Communication Capabilities Using a global cellular network, the SpotBot GL offers superior power efficiency and signal reach for monitoring assets worldwide. It can also connect to recognized WiFi networks, offering flexibility in global communication. User-Friendly Operation Setting up the SpotBot GL is simple with its intuitive web application. Trip data is securely stored in the SpotSee Cloud and can be easily downloaded, providing detailed information on the time, location, and impact severity experienced during transit.

Về dòng SpotBot

SpotBot là thiết bị giám sát kết nối (4G LTE-M, Wi-Fi hoặc mạng toàn cầu GL) theo dõi va đập 3 trục, nhiệt độ, độ ẩm và vị trí. Dữ liệu và cảnh báo hiển thị trên SpotSee Cloud trong suốt hành trình.

  • Va đập 3 trục, nhiệt độ, độ ẩm, GPS
  • SpotBot GL cho tuyến quốc tế, 4G Plus cho nội địa Mỹ
  • Kèm gói dịch vụ 12 tháng
  • Gá nam châm tháo lắp nhanh
