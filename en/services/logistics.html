Import & Logistics

End-to-end import management: international freight, Vietnamese customs clearance and complete specialised documentation.

Full import management

From factory gate to your site — fully managed

Importing industrial equipment into Vietnam involves more than international freight. Vietnamese customs requires accurate HS code classification, import licences for certain product categories, full commercial documentation and — for project equipment — the CO/CQ certificates and technical papers that project acceptance demands.

Fast Group manages this process completely on behalf of customers. We coordinate with freight forwarders, handle customs declarations, resolve any inspection or duty queries and ensure the documentation package is complete for both customs release and project records.

What we manage

Import, customs and documentation — every step

International freight

Air, sea and express courier coordination from manufacturer warehouse to Vietnam port or airport, with tracking and schedule updates.

Customs clearance

HS code classification, import duty assessment, customs declaration filing and resolution of inspection requirements.

Documentation package

Commercial invoice, packing list, CO/CQ, datasheet, test certificates and any project-specific documents — compiled and delivered with every shipment.

Last-mile delivery

Domestic transport from customs release to your warehouse, project site or offshore installation — coordinated with your schedule.

Questions about importing to Vietnam?

Contact us with your product details and we will advise on import requirements, documentation and logistics lead times.
