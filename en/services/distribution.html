Supply & Distribution

Authorised distributor — genuine supply, complete documentation and on-schedule delivery for your project or maintenance order.

Genuine supply from the source

Working directly with manufacturers — the buyer's advantage

Fast Group supplies industrial equipment directly sourced from the brands we represent. Every item is genuine, traceable and supported by the full manufacturer documentation set. Direct manufacturer relationships mean we confirm availability faster, secure better pricing and maintain priority allocation for our customers' orders.

We do not simply forward enquiries. Every request receives a technical review: we confirm the correct model for the application, verify availability and lead time, and prepare a complete quotation with pricing, delivery schedule and the documentation package required for project acceptance.

What we guarantee

Genuine, accurate, complete and on time

Genuine supply

All equipment sourced directly from the manufacturer or authorised chain — verified authentic with full traceability.

Accurate quotation

Correct model identification, confirmed lead time and accurate pricing before any order commitment — no surprises after PO.

Complete documentation

CO/CQ certificates, datasheets, packing lists and project-specific technical paperwork — delivered with every shipment.

On-schedule delivery

Import, customs and last-mile logistics actively managed so equipment arrives at your site when your project needs it.

Supporting vendors & contractors

Supply support across the project chain

Fast Group actively supports vendors, suppliers, agents and EPC contractors who need access to genuine industrial equipment, competitive quotations and complete technical documentation to serve their own customers and projects.

Our role is that of a back-end supply partner: we provide equipment, pricing and paperwork transparently, respect each partner's existing customer relationships, and do not compete in end-user accounts where partners are already engaged. That clarity of role is what makes the arrangement work for everyone.

We supply and support

  • EPC contractors requiring multi-brand consolidated supply
  • Vendors and suppliers needing genuine equipment for their customers
  • Maintenance teams sourcing spare parts and MRO materials
  • Procurement teams requiring CO/CQ documentation for project acceptance
  • Engineers needing application-level technical selection support
