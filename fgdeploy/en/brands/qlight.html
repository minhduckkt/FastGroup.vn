About Qlight

Headquarters

Sector
Industrial signal & warning devices

Founded in 1986 in South Korea, Qlight has grown into one of Asia's leading manufacturers of industrial signalling and safety devices. Their product range spans conventional and LED signal towers, multi-function alarm sounders, rotating and strobe beacons, and integrated signal tower/sounder combinations — all designed and certified for industrial environments.

Qlight products include the popular SEN and SCL signal tower families, qSeries modern LED towers, and the EXSBT ATEX/IECEx-certified explosion-proof signal tower — widely used on offshore platforms, chemical plants and hazardous area control panels in Vietnam's oil & gas and petrochemical sectors.

Key strengths

  • Full range from standard to ATEX Zone 1/Zone 2 certified
  • LED technology for long life and low maintenance
  • Wide range of tones, colours and combination units
  • IP66/IP67 rated — suitable for outdoor and wash-down environments
