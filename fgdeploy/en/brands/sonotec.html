About SONOTEC

Founded
1991
Headquarters
Halle (Saale), Germany
Sector
Ultrasonic technology

Founded in 1991 in Halle (Saale), Germany, SONOTEC has specialised in ultrasonic sensor technology and measurement instruments for over 30 years. Their products are used in industrial facilities worldwide for compressed air and gas leak detection (SONAPHONE series), non-destructive thickness gauging and weld inspection, and non-invasive clamp-on flow measurement for liquids and gases.

SONOTEC's SONAPHONE ultrasonic detectors are particularly valued in the Oil & Gas and petrochemical sectors for detecting leaks in pressurised piping, valves and flange joints — enabling maintenance teams to find leaks quickly, even in high-noise plant environments. Their clamp-on flow meters require no pipe cutting and can be installed while the line is in service.

Key strengths

  • 30+ year German ultrasonic technology expertise
  • SONAPHONE — industry-leading portable leak detector
  • Non-invasive clamp-on flow meters: no shutdown, no pipe cutting
  • ATEX-certified versions available for hazardous area leak detection
