Diffu-Therm

Origin: Herten, Germany

Diffu-Therm is the non-destructive testing brand of Helmut Klumpf Technische Chemie KG (Herten, Germany) — the manufacturer behind the oldest German red/white penetrant testing system. The range covers dye penetrants, developers and cleaners for PT, magnetic powders and suspensions for MT, UV fluorescent systems, and the System C range that works on surfaces up to 200 °C.

Fast Group distributes genuine Diffu-Therm products in Vietnam. We work directly with the supply source to guarantee authenticity and batch traceability, and every shipment comes with the SDS/manuals and CO/CQ paperwork the project needs. With NDT consumables this is not a detail: a missing English SDS or a CO/CQ that does not match the batch number is enough to hold up an inspection sign-off.

Diffu-Therm did not start out as an ordinary chemicals brand. The penetrant method that carries the name was developed at the Junkers aircraft and engine works in Dessau and patented in Germany in 1943 (DRP 895.839), with Helmut Klumpf credited as the inventor. An improved version was patented again in 1958 (DBP 1.207.663). The company has manufactured independently in Herten since 1950, and Diffu-Therm is known today as the oldest German red/white penetrant testing system.

That history matters in practice for QA/QC teams. This is a manufacturer that has done one thing for more than seven decades, and the range is built as a matched system — penetrant, developer and cleaner from the same family are tested to work together. It is the reason serious NDT procedures discourage mixing consumables from two different suppliers within a single inspection.

Key strengths

  • Matched red/white PT system: penetrant, developer and cleaner from one family
  • UV fluorescent system for high sensitivity on very fine defects
  • System C range for inspection on hot surfaces up to 200 °C
  • Magnetic powders and suspensions for MT, in black and fluorescent versions
  • Documentation referencing DIN EN ISO 3452, ISO 9934 and ASME Section V
