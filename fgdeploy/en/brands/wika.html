About WIKA

Founded
1946
Headquarters
Klingenberg am Main, Germany
Sector
Pressure & temperature measurement

WIKA Alexander Wiegand SE & Co. KG is widely known as a German specialist in pressure and temperature instrumentation. Its portfolio spans mechanical pressure gauges, pressure transmitters, switches, temperature instruments, thermowells and mounting accessories.

Fast Group supports customers in Vietnam with part-number checking, specification matching, RFQ preparation and supply of WIKA instruments for maintenance, replacement and project requirements. A dedicated WIKA brand portal is also available on the website for deeper product lookup.

Key strengths

  • Broad pressure and temperature instrumentation range
  • Many process connections, materials and measuring ranges
  • Suitable for replacement by part number or nameplate
  • Dedicated WIKA portal for deeper product lookup
