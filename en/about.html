About Fast Group

Working directly with manufacturers

Founded in 2019, Fast Group is an authorised industrial equipment distributor in Vietnam. Our defining strength is direct working relationships with manufacturers — giving us control over supply, pricing, lead times and the authenticity of every order.

We do more than buy and resell. Fast Group positions itself as a trusted link in the industrial supply chain: securing the best supply source, preparing complete documentation, and staying with the customer until each order and project is closed.

With headquarters in Ho Chi Minh City, a branch office in Vung Tau — the centre of Vietnam's oil & gas industry — and a presence in Singapore, we are well placed to connect international supply with domestic industrial demand.

Competitive advantage

Working directly with manufacturers — the buyer's advantage

Direct manufacturer relationships deliver clear advantages: preferential pricing and supply policies, guaranteed genuine stock, shorter lead times and direct access to technical information from source. In competitive industrial tenders and project orders, these factors are decisive.

For every enquiry, we identify the correct part number, verify supply availability and respond with a fast, accurate quotation — helping customers and partners stay in control of cost and schedule.

Partnership philosophy

Ready to support, respecting our partners' role

Fast Group understands that many orders and projects are completed through networks of vendors, suppliers, agents and contractors. We are consistently ready to support partners in accessing genuine supply, receiving accurate quotations and product information, and obtaining the documentation they need to serve their own customers.

Fast Group's role is that of a back-end supply partner: providing goods, information and paperwork transparently while respecting each partner's existing customer relationships. That clarity and reliability is the foundation of long-term partnerships.
