QTG60(M)L-WIZ trong hệ thống tín hiệu công nghiệp

QTG60(M)L-WIZ là model Qlight thuộc QTG-WIZ Series trong Đèn tháp tín hiệu không dây Qlight. Ø60mm Wireless LED Steady/ Flashing Tower Light Max.85dB.

Fast Group Engineering là nhà phân phối được Qlight cấp Certificate of Distributor, cung cấp QTG60(M)L-WIZ chính hãng tại Việt Nam cho nhà máy, nhà thầu EPC và đơn vị tích hợp hệ thống: đối chiếu cấu hình theo đúng mã đặt hàng, kiểm tra tồn kho và lead time, nhập khẩu kèm CO/CQ.

Tính năng theo catalog Qlight

  • Ideal solution for building smart factories with state of the art technology
  • Wireless monitoring and control of signal tower light via PC or smartphone
  • WIZ series can integrate mismatch among old machineries, latest equipment and various brands
  • Possible to control the product via external communication and PC application software
  • The product transfers visual and audible warning signals as well as data being monitored remotely by means of wireless communication.
  • Productivity and failure rate management for each machine
  • Cost effective solution for an advanced manufacturing network system
  • Mesh topology uses 2.4GHz local wireless telecommunication to enable stable communication even under the harsh environmental conditions.
  • The multi-hop feature allows for long distance communication up-to 10 HOP (100 meter between the units).
  • Production equipment relocation would be convenient because the wireless network system does not require a telecommunication cable.
  • 5 built-in buzzer tones are available.
  • Compliant with RoHS directive
  • OS Support: Windows 7 or later
  • Library Support: C#(64bit only)
  • Wireless communication protocol : Zigbee(IEEE802.15.4)
  • 2.4GHz radio communication available
