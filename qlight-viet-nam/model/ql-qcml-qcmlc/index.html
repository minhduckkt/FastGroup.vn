Fast Group Engineering là nhà phân phối được Qlight cấp Certificate of Distributor, cung cấp QCML/QCMLC chính hãng tại Việt Nam cho nhà máy, nhà thầu EPC và đơn vị tích hợp hệ thống: đối chiếu cấu hình theo đúng mã đặt hàng, kiểm tra tồn kho và lead time, nhập khẩu kèm CO/CQ.

  • Side cover and lens is integrated together with a multi silicon rubber sealing structure for superior protection from water
  • Aesthetically pleasing with slim and compact housing
  • Polycarbonate material provides superior heat and shock resistance
  • Features great seal and durability in food processing, pharmaceutical, and beverage processing machine that needs high temperature wash down
  • Can select from light colors of Cooldaylight, warm white, amber and red color lights depending on your application needs such as semiconductor manufacturing equipment, machine tools and test equipment

  • Protection rating: IP67, IP69K
  • Standard entry cable included. Can customize the product with the M12 connector if requested(Female M12 connector is not provided)
