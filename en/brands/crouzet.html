Crouzet

Origin: France

Crouzet is a French specialist in automation and control components — relays, timers, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), sensors and micro-motors used in industrial machines and systems worldwide.

Fast Group distributes Crouzet components in Vietnam, providing genuine supply, technical selection support and documentation for industrial automation projects.

  • Electromechanical and solid-state relays for control panels

About Crouzet

Founded
1921
Headquarters
Valence, France
Sector
Automation & control components

Founded in 1921 in Valence, France, Crouzet has over a century of engineering heritage in the design and manufacture of control and automation components. The company's products are trusted across industrial automation, aerospace, medical equipment and building automation sectors. Crouzet is part of the Schneider Electric ecosystem through its parent group.

Crouzet's product portfolio covers electromechanical relays, solid-state relays, timers, Millenium 3 programmable controllers, inductive and photoelectric sensors, and high-efficiency micro-motors. Their components are designed for long service life in demanding industrial environments.

Key strengths

  • 100+ years of French engineering heritage
  • Compact, high-reliability components for industrial environments
  • Millenium 3 programmable controllers with easy programming
  • Broad sensor portfolio: inductive, photoelectric, capacitive
