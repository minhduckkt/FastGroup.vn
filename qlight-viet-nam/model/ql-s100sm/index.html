Tính năng theo catalog Qlight

  • Ø100mm xenon lamp strobe signal light
  • Power supply through cigarette lighter plug
  • It has high instantaneous luminous efficiency and emits signal light close to natural light, providing excellent long-distance visibility
  • Lamp connector structure for convenient lamp replacement
  • Flashing rate : 60-80 flashes/min
  • Ambient operating temperature : -30°C to +50°C
