About Hansford Sensors

Founded
2006
Headquarters
Buckinghamshire, United Kingdom
Sector
Vibration sensors & condition monitoring

Founded in 2006 by Chris Hansford — who brought over 35 years of vibration measurement and condition monitoring experience — Hansford Sensors was designed from the outset to become an international centre of excellence in vibration monitoring. The company designs and manufactures high-quality accelerometers, vibration transmitters and temperature sensors for industrial machinery.

Hansford Sensors' range covers 4-20mA vibration transmitters for continuous online monitoring, 100mV/g accelerometers for data collector and portable analyser use, and a full range of mounting accessories. Their products are used to monitor motors, pumps, fans, compressors and other rotating equipment in power stations, oil & gas facilities and manufacturing plants.

Key strengths

  • UK-designed and manufactured industrial vibration sensors
  • Full range: 4-20mA transmitters, 100mV/g, IEPE accelerometers
  • IP67/IP68 rated — suitable for harsh plant environments
  • Complete accessory range: mounting bases, cables, junction boxes
