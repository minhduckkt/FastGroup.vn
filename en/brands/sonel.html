About Sonel

Founded
1994
Headquarters

Sector
Electrical measurement & safety testing

Founded in 1994 in Świdnica, Poland, Sonel has grown to become one of Europe's leading manufacturers of electrical measurement instruments. Their product range covers MIC earth and insulation resistance testers, MZC loop impedance and short-circuit current testers, power quality analysers, PAT testers and thermographic cameras — all designed and certified to IEC safety testing standards.

Sonel instruments are used by electrical engineers, safety inspectors and commissioning teams to verify insulation integrity, earthing system compliance, power quality and thermal performance of electrical installations. Their products comply with IEC 61557, EN 61010 and associated electrical safety testing standards.

Key strengths

  • European-certified electrical safety testing instruments
  • Comprehensive range: earth, insulation, loop, power quality in one brand
  • IEC 61557 and EN 61010 compliant
  • Lightweight portable design with memory and PC download capability
