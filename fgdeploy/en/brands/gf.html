About Georg Fischer (GF Piping Systems)

Founded
1802
Headquarters
Schaffhausen, Switzerland
Sector
Piping systems & fluid control

Founded in 1802, Georg Fischer is one of the world's oldest industrial technology companies. GF Piping Systems, the largest division, supplies thermoplastic piping systems and fluid handling components — from CPVC and PVDF pipe and fittings to automated valves, flow meters and sensors — that replace metallic systems in corrosive, ultra-pure and aggressive service environments.

GF products are the system of choice where corrosion-resistance, chemical compatibility and long service life are critical: semiconductor fabs, chemical plants, desalination facilities and industrial utilities. Their actuated valves and flow meters integrate readily with plant DCS and SCADA systems.

Key strengths

  • 220+ year Swiss engineering heritage
  • Comprehensive system: pipe, fittings, valves and instrumentation in one brand
  • PVDF and CPVC options for aggressive chemical resistance
  • Full fusion welding system for leak-free installation
