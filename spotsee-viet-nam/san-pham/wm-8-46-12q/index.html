WarmMark QR: A Smart, Connected Solution for Cold Chain MonitoringThe WarmMark QR is a single-use, battery-free temperature sensor that revolutionizes cold chain monitoring. Enabled by smartphones, it effortlessly detects temperature excursions, providing essential data like time, date, location, and serial number. This information is uploaded to the SpotSee Cloud, delivering actionable insights to improve shipping quality and efficiency. Designed to provide clear, visual indicators, the WarmMark QR changes from white to pink/red when unacceptably high temperatures are detected. With its simplicity and reliability, it ensures that cold chain stakeholders—from manufacturers to patients—have real-time visibility into the condition of temperature-sensitive products. Key Benefits:Improve Decision Making: Gain insights to identify, analyze, and enhance cold chain lanes or problem areas remotely. Visualize Cold Chain Performance: Track sensitive assets with real-time, cloud-based monitoring, ensuring visibility down to the patient level. When your customer or patient knows, so do you. Compliance & Data Security: Built to comply with 21 CFR Part 11 requirements, WarmMark QR ensures secure access and regulatory adherence for shipment data. Adoption Made Easy: No app required—WarmMark QR is user-friendly for all cold chain personnel, from manufacturers to end recipients. How It Works:Prepare and Apply: Activate the WarmMark QR sensor and apply it to your shipment. Monitor Temperatures

Về dòng WarmMark QR

WarmMark QR kết hợp chỉ thị nhiệt độ WarmMark với mã QR định danh. Khi người nhận quét bằng điện thoại, trạng thái, thời gian, vị trí và số serial được tải lên SpotSee Cloud, tạo hồ sơ điện tử cho từng kiện hàng.

  • Dùng một lần, không pin; chuyển từ trắng sang hồng/đỏ khi vượt ngưỡng
  • Quét bằng điện thoại, không cần app riêng
  • Dữ liệu tập trung trên SpotSee Cloud, hỗ trợ truy vết
  • Nhiều ngưỡng và thời gian: −18 °C 12 h, 0 °C 48 h, 5 °C 8 h, 8 °C 8 h…
