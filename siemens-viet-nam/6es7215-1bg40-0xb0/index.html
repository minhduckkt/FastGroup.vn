Siemens 6ES7215-1BG40-0XB0 là CPU PLC thuộc dòng SIMATIC S7-1200. Mô tả đặt hàng theo Siemens: SIMATIC S7-1200, CPU 1215C, compact CPU, AC/DC/relay, 2 PROFINET ports, onboard I/O: 14 DI 24 V DC; 10 DO relay 2 A, 2 AI 0-10 V DC, 2 AO 0-20 mA DC, power supply: AC 85-264 V AC at 47-63 Hz, program/data memory 200 KB.
