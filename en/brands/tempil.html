About Tempil® / Tempilstik®

Founded
c. 1937
Headquarters
USA (LA-CO Industries)
Sector
Temperature-indicating products

Tempil has been manufacturing temperature-indicating products since approximately 1937 and is now a brand of LA-CO Industries. Tempilstik® temperature-indicating crayons are the standard tool for welding preheat and interpass temperature verification worldwide — used on pipelines, pressure vessels, structural steel and any application where temperature control is specified by welding procedure specifications (WPS).

Tempil products are available in over 100 specific temperatures from 38°C to 1371°C. Each Tempilstik® melts at a defined temperature, providing an immediate, irreversible visual indication that the required preheat or interpass temperature has been reached. Temp-Plates® self-adhesive labels provide a permanent, documented heat record for quality assurance purposes.

Key strengths

  • 85+ year heritage — the global standard for weld preheat measurement
  • Available in 100+ specific temperatures: 38°C to 1371°C
  • Immediate, irreversible visual indication — no instruments required
  • Accepted per AWS, ASME, EN and API welding standards
