About the brand

About HYDAC

Founded
1963
Headquarters
Sulzbach/Saar, Germany
Sector
Hydraulics & fluid filtration

HYDAC is a German specialist in hydraulics and fluid management, with strong product ranges in hydraulic filtration, accumulators, sensors, cooling and power-unit assemblies. HYDAC products are widely used in steel plants, oil & gas, power, cement, presses and mobile equipment.

Fast Group helps customers identify the correct HYDAC filter element, sensor, accumulator or accessory based on part number, operating conditions and project documentation requirements.

Key strengths

  • Deep hydraulics and oil-filtration portfolio
  • Common for machinery maintenance and power units
  • Wide sensor and fluid-monitoring options
  • Replacement support for existing filter-element codes
