Magnaflux

Origin: United States

Magnaflux is a pioneer and global leader in non-destructive testing (NDT) — producing magnetic particle (MT) and liquid penetrant (PT) inspection systems, equipment and consumables for weld and component inspection.

Fast Group supplies Magnaflux NDT products in Vietnam, supporting QA/QC departments, welding inspection teams and EPC contractors in oil & gas, power and heavy industry.

Typical applications

  • Weld inspection using magnetic particle testing (MT) on pipelines and vessels
  • Dye penetrant (PT) testing for surface crack detection on castings and welds
  • Fluorescent MT and PT for high-sensitivity aerospace and power-generation inspection
  • QA/QC procedures per ASME, API and AWS inspection codes

About Magnaflux

Founded
1934
Headquarters
Illinois, USA
Sector
Non-destructive testing (NDT)

Founded in 1934 in Illinois, Magnaflux invented magnetic particle inspection and has been at the forefront of surface-defect NDT ever since. Their MT and PT systems are trusted by aerospace, oil & gas, power generation, automotive and heavy equipment manufacturers globally for critical weld and casting inspection.

Magnaflux's product range includes portable and stationary magnetic particle inspection benches, fluorescent and visible penetrant inspection kits, UV lamp systems and Spotcheck® dye penetrant products — all formulated and certified for ASME, API, AWS and MIL-SPEC inspection procedures.

Key strengths

  • 90-year NDT heritage — invented magnetic particle inspection
  • Spotcheck® and Zyglo® — globally recognised PT and MT product lines
  • Full system: equipment, consumables and UV lamps from one supplier
  • Products qualified to ASME, API, AWS, Nadcap and MIL-SPEC
